One workflow. Four records, each with its own owner.

Capture facts with Deviation Companion, investigate causes with Deviation Investigator, manage affected items with Impact & Disposition, and review corrective or preventive actions with CAPA. Display order is not a compulsory sequence.

Before we talk.

A few practical questions.

What can we pilot?

Choose the Deviation/CAPA workflow or the SOP workflow. We agree the scope and availability for your chosen pilot.

Pilots are scoped and priced per site. They run on synthetic or customer-approved data within agreed boundaries. Contact hello@verrionsystems.com to discuss.

Where does AI fit?

AI can assist with selected analysis and drafting tasks after the applicable data review. Authorised people review the output and own the decisions. Impact & Disposition uses no AI.

What would a walkthrough cover?

We start with your quality workflow, then explore the relevant modules, who reviews the records and what evidence they need. A pilot can use synthetic or customer-approved scenarios within agreed data boundaries.

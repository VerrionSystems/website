# Defensible Deviation Investigation Checklist

Use this as a private-demo and pilot-support asset for Verrion Deviation Investigator. It is not legal, regulatory, or validation advice.

## 1. Event definition

- The deviation is described in clear, factual language.
- The process, product, batch, equipment, material, site, and time window are identified where applicable.
- Severity/category is assigned using the customer’s approved process.
- Initial containment or immediate actions are recorded.

## 2. Evidence pack completeness

- Batch records, equipment logs, environmental/process trends, SOP extracts, intervention notes, and relevant communications are linked.
- Missing evidence is visible rather than buried in narrative text.
- Evidence is tied to investigation questions, not just uploaded as attachments.
- Related batches, products, equipment, materials, and time windows have been considered.

## 3. Investigation reasoning

- Plausible causes are distinguished from confirmed root cause.
- Human error is not accepted as the endpoint without checking system, process, training, equipment, material, and control factors.
- Control gaps are described explicitly.
- Assumptions are visible and challengeable.
- Evidence gaps are reviewed before conclusions are approved.

## 4. RCA/CAPA quality

- CAPA rationale links back to the failed or weak control.
- CAPA does not only address the symptom.
- Proposed actions are proportionate to product-quality, patient-safety, compliance, and recurrence risk.
- Effectiveness checks are considered where applicable.
- Ownership and due dates are clear.

## 5. AI governance where AI is used

- Sensitive identifiers are sanitised before AI support in hosted workflows.
- AI suggestions are accepted, edited, or rejected by an accountable human user.
- The investigation record shows what was suggested and what was used.
- AI does not make the QA decision.
- Model/prompt version or equivalent context is recorded where available.

## 6. Review readiness

- The final narrative can answer obvious QA/reviewer/inspector challenge questions.
- Reviewer comments and investigator responses are captured.
- Audit trail shows who did what, when, and why.
- Export output is complete enough for review and record retention decisions.
- Any production GxP use is governed by the customer’s intended-use validation process.

# V2 Module Subpages Design

## Goal

Create two V2 subpages that explain the Deviation Investigator and SOP Intelligence modules in more depth, while protecting proprietary implementation detail. The pages should persuade Pharma Leaders and Professionals that Verrion Systems understands both GMP quality risk and AI governance risk at a level beyond generic AI vendors or broad compliance-suite messaging.

## Naming Rule

Use **Verrion Systems** consistently. Do not shorten the company name to "Verrion" in public-facing copy because another company uses that name.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- QA Leaders
- QPs
- Deviation Investigators
- SOP Owners
- Quality Systems Leaders
- Operations SMEs involved in GMP investigations

The tone should be confident, precise, inspection-aware and commercially credible. Avoid hype, founder mythology, chatbot language and "secret sauce" hints.

## Positioning

Use the approved credibility frame:

> Verrion Systems is QP-led and AI-native: built from practical GMP quality experience, with a deep understanding of how AI must be controlled, reviewed, challenged and evidenced in regulated work.

The founder/QP point should be visible as a trust anchor, but not the whole sales story. It belongs in a "Built from inside regulated quality" or "Why Verrion Systems" section rather than the hero headline.

## Page 1: Deviation Investigator

Recommended headline direction:

> Deviation investigations that can withstand review.

Core message:

Weak investigations usually fail because the reasoning is unsupported: evidence is detached from conclusions, SOP expectations arrive too late, human error becomes a shallow endpoint, CAPA addresses symptoms, and AI-generated text cannot be reconstructed. Verrion Systems Deviation Investigator should be positioned as a controlled workspace for making those gaps visible before approval.

Suggested sections:

- Hero: investigation defensibility, evidence discipline and controlled AI support.
- Why investigations fail: weak causal logic, missing evidence, unchallenged assumptions, poor CAPA linkage.
- What the module helps teams control: event facts, evidence pack, SOP context, impact questions, RCA/CAPA rationale, reviewer decisions and exportable review pack.
- AI support with human accountability: sanitisation, prompt/model versioning, accept/edit/reject, AI-assisted content flags.
- Review-ready output: evidence links, reasoning trail, audit history and decision state.
- QP-led / AI-native credibility: built by people who understand batch-release accountability and modern AI risk.
- CTA: private walkthrough or pilot discussion.

Do not reveal implementation logic, prompt strategy, scoring methods, analysis internals, or proprietary governance matrix detail.

## Page 2: SOP Intelligence

Recommended headline direction:

> SOP expectations visible before quality reasoning drifts.

Core message:

SOPs should act as working controls, not static PDFs consulted after conclusions are drafted. Verrion Systems SOP Intelligence should be positioned as a controlled way to interrogate procedure content, extract expectations, compare them with investigation context, and surface ambiguity or control gaps for human review.

Suggested sections:

- Hero: SOPs as live quality context, not document storage.
- Why SOPs fail as working controls: ambiguity, stale expectations, manual searching, procedure drift, missing escalation logic.
- What the module helps teams see: clause-linked expectations, procedure-to-investigation alignment, possible gaps, questions for reviewers.
- AI-assisted interpretation with controls: sanitised context, traceability, human review, exportable notes.
- Connection to Deviation Investigator: SOP Intelligence strengthens investigation reasoning rather than replacing quality judgement.
- QP-led / AI-native credibility: practical GMP quality experience plus serious AI control awareness.
- CTA: private walkthrough or pilot discussion.

Do not expose detailed prompt flows, extraction strategy, ranking logic, or internal analysis methods.

## Layout Direction

The subpages should feel like part of the V2 prototype:

- Use the same header, footer, typography, buttons, grid background and restrained navy/teal palette.
- Prefer open layouts, timelines, comparison rows and evidence-led panels over repeated generic card grids.
- Use module-specific product surfaces that look like controlled work records rather than chatbots.
- Keep copy concise and specific; every section should answer a regulated-quality buyer concern.
- Provide links between the two module pages and back to the V2 homepage.

## Success Criteria

- Two mobile-responsive V2 subpages exist and can be opened from the V2 homepage.
- Public copy uses "Verrion Systems" consistently.
- The pages communicate AI competence and GMP quality credibility without overclaiming validation or regulatory compliance.
- The pages avoid revealing proprietary mechanics.
- Local preview remains available for remote review.
- Browser QA passes at desktop, tablet and mobile widths with no clipped text, overlap, horizontal scroll, or console errors.

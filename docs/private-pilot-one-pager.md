# Verrion Deviation Investigator — Private Pilot One-Pager

## Purpose

Verrion Deviation Investigator is a controlled investigation workspace for GMP quality teams evaluating how to improve deviation investigation quality, evidence discipline, RCA/CAPA rationale, and review readiness.

It is not positioned as a replacement for an existing QMS during pilot evaluation. It supports the investigation work around the quality event: structuring evidence, surfacing gaps, documenting reasoning, keeping AI suggestions reviewable, and preparing outputs for QA review.

## Best-fit pilot users

- QA leaders
- QPs / quality leads
- Approved deviation investigators
- Operations SMEs
- Supervisors, team leaders, and operations managers supporting GMP investigations

## Typical pilot scope

- Four-week fixed-scope engagement
- One to two synthetic or customer-approved deviation packs
- Small QA user group with guided onboarding
- Hosted by Verrion with AWS Bedrock in eu-west-2 for AI analysis
- Mandatory sanitisation before AI support
- End-of-pilot findings review plus export/deletion path

## What the pilot evaluates

- Whether the workflow improves investigation structure
- Whether evidence gaps are easier to identify before approval
- Whether RCA/CAPA rationale is clearer and more defensible
- Whether AI suggestions are useful when kept human-reviewed, editable, and rejectable
- Whether the data-handling and validation-readiness posture is suitable for deeper customer review

## Demo flow

1. Walk through a synthetic deviation pack.
2. Review sanitisation before AI support.
3. Build the investigation frame from evidence, SOP extracts, and supporting documents.
4. Surface impact questions, possible control gaps, and unresolved evidence needs.
5. Review AI suggestions with accept/edit/reject controls.
6. Link CAPA rationale to evidence and failed controls.
7. Review audit trail, export state, and pilot-fit boundaries.

## Regulated-use posture

Verrion is designed with awareness of GMP quality workflows, data integrity expectations, audit trails, electronic record/e-signature posture, and validation-readiness needs. It does not claim customer validation by default.

Customers remain responsible for validating any system for their own intended GxP use before production deployment.

## Pilot outcome

At the end of the pilot, the customer should have a clear view of:

- workflow fit;
- investigation-quality impact;
- user acceptance;
- data-control suitability;
- validation and governance questions for any next step.

Where SOPs lose control value

Procedures can be approved, current and still hard to use during a live investigation.

01

Expectations are scattered across long documents.

Investigators lose time searching for clauses while the reasoning already starts to form elsewhere.

02

Ambiguity hides inside familiar wording.

Phrases that feel obvious during routine work can become weak controls when evidence, timing or escalation is challenged.

03

Procedure drift appears after repeated deviations.

Investigation patterns can show that the SOP no longer reflects how work is controlled in practice.

04

AI interpretation lacks review discipline.

Procedure summaries are risky if teams cannot inspect the source clause, support context and reviewer decision.
